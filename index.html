About Us

Talk & Tea Café is Founded by Devansh Pokhariya in 2005. At Talk & Tea, we believe that every great conversation begins with a perfect cup of chai. Our café was created with a simple vision — to bring the authentic taste of North Indian chai and snacks to one cosy place where people can relax, connect, and enjoy real flavours. From freshly brewed masala chai to crispy samosas, kachori, puffs, dahi papdi chaat, namkeen, and biscuits, every item on our menu is prepared with care, quality ingredients, and traditional recipes. We focus on freshness, taste, and consistency so that every visit feels like home. Whether you're here for a quick tea break, an evening snack, or a long chat with friends, Talk & Tea is your everyday escape for comfort, flavour, and warmth. Come for the chai, stay for the vibes.

Our Menu

  • Samosa

    Crispy on the outside, spicy on the inside — our samosas are pure comfort in every bite. Filled with flavourful mashed potatoes, peas, and secret spices. Deep-fried to golden perfection and served hot. Best enjoyed with our tangy chutneys and a cup of chai.

  • Chai or Tea

    Brewed with premium tea leaves and fragrant Indian spices. Rich, strong and soul-warming in every sip. Made the traditional way for the perfect balance of taste and aroma. Because every great conversation begins with chai.

  • Kachori

    A crunchy shell packed with bold, aromatic stuffing that bursts with flavour. Perfectly spiced and fried until irresistibly crisp. Every bite delivers a satisfying crunch with rich Indian taste. A true snack lover’s favourite.

  • Namkeen

    Crunchy, savoury and full of flavour — the perfect anytime snack. A delightful mix of spices and textures in every handful. Pairs wonderfully with chai or as a light munch. Once you start, you won’t stop!

  • Dahi Papdi Chat

    A delicious mix of crispy papdi, soft potatoes, chilled curd and spicy chutneys. Sweet, tangy and spicy flavours come together in perfect harmony. Topped with crunchy sev for that extra bite. A refreshing street-style treat you’ll crave again and again.

  • Biscuits

    Fresh, crunchy and lightly sweet — our biscuits are baked to perfection. Perfect companion for your morning and evening chai.
